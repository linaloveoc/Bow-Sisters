My Hero Academia archive

Lina Senju

A Portuguese-Japanese U.A. student in Class 2-A, Lina is a healing and energy-based combat/support specialist who trains hard enough to stand on the front line herself. Her story follows friendship, heroism, trauma, love, and the life she builds beyond U.A.

Character file

Hero, healer, protector

Lina’s early life is tied to Reid Anderson, the first person she healed when her Quirk manifested. After moving to Japan, she grows up alongside Mina Ashido and Eijiro Kirishima, attends U.A., and ultimately becomes a Pro Hero.

Canon-divergent MHA continuity: U.A., hero society, major conflicts, post-war development, and Lina’s place among established characters.

Relationships

Childhood bonds with Mina and Kirishima, an unresolved history with Reid, mentor Hayato Suwa, and her established relationship with Katsuki Bakugo.

AUs

Reid Reunion, Future Pro Hero, KatsuLina Family, College Sports, Mafia, hybrid, domestic, and MHA × KNY crossover timelines.

Demon Slayer archive

Sumire Kanzaki

Known as the Ribbon Hashira, Sumire is a poised but formidable swordswoman who created Ribbon Breathing from the structural strength of Stone and the fluid adaptability of Water. Her story is shaped by grief, survival, quiet strength, and the people she learns to trust.

Character file

The Ribbon Hashira

Rescued by Gyomei after the destruction of her mountain village, Sumire trains under Urokodaki alongside Giyu Tomioka, develops a derivative breathing style recognized by Kagaya Ubuyashiki, and rises to Hashira after defeating a Kizuki alone.

Alternate universes

Stories beyond canon

MHA × KNY Crossover

A Quirk accident transports Lina and Katsuki into the Demon Slayer era, where Sumire and Sanemi initially mistake their abilities for demonic powers. The collision of their worlds begins with conflict and becomes an unlikely alliance.

Lina AU Collection

From a no-Quirk Mafia assassin known as Ghost to a college cheerleader, adult Pro Hero, mother, or visitor in another world, Lina remains fiercely protective in every version.

Sumire AU Collection

Stories that ask who Sumire becomes when tragedy changes: a teacher, healer, princess, college student, hybrid, or someone granted the future canon denied her.

Rengoku Lives / Twins AU

In a gentler branch, Kyojuro survives. Sumire and Kyojuro build a life together and raise twins Hime and Kenjuro, combining Flame and Ribbon traditions.
